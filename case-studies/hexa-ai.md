# Hexa AI

![Hexa AI overview](../assets/hexa-ai.svg)

A platform for businesses to handle inbound and outbound calls using AI voice agents. It supports custom conversation flows and role-based access.

[Visit the product](https://gethexa.ai/)

## Technology

NestJS · Next.js · Twilio · OpenAI · ElevenLabs · PostgreSQL

## Product scope

- Inbound + outbound calls
- Conversation flows
- Business integrations
